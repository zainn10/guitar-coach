## The famous one

Full F is the chord that makes people quit guitar. It doesn't have to be. The trick is to build it in stages over a week or two, never squeezing for minutes at a time.

## Stage A: easy F (you know it)

::chord Fmaj7

## Stage B: small F, a two-string barre

Your index finger lies flat across the **1st and 2nd strings** at the 1st fret. Four strings only.

::build Fsmall

## Stage C: full F

The index finger covers all six strings. Middle, ring and little fingers make an E shape on top.

::build F

## Making it ring

::mistake barre-roll

1. **Right behind the fret wire**, closer than feels natural.
2. **Roll the index finger** slightly onto its bony outer edge.
3. **Arm weight, not thumb squeeze**: pull the guitar body gently into you with your right forearm. The neck comes into your left hand.
4. **Check string by string.** Usually only one or two strings are dead; move the barre up or down a millimetre to shift a joint crease off them.
5. **Short reps**: 5 attempts of 10 seconds, then rest. Daily.

::change C F

::trouble f-chord barre-dead hand-ache
