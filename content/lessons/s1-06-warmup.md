## One finger per fret

Your left hand gets one fret per finger: index on the 1st fret, middle on the 2nd, ring on the 3rd, little on the 4th. This exercise uses every finger evenly and teaches you to press cleanly.

::tab bpm=60 per=1 title=Warm-up_on_the_6th_and_5th_strings
e|---------------------------|
B|---------------------------|
G|---------------------------|
D|---------------------------|
A|-------------1--2--3--4----|
E|-1--2--3--4----------------|
::end

## How to press a clean note

- Press with the **very tip** of the finger, nail pointing up.
- Place it **just behind the fret wire**, almost touching it.
- Press only **as hard as needed**: start light, add pressure until the buzz stops.
- Pluck with your right thumb, downwards.

::mistake far-from-fret

## Do it with the metronome

One note per click, slow and even. When it's clean at 60, try 80.

::metronome bpm=60

::tip Leave each finger down as you add the next one on the same string (1, then 1+2, then 1+2+3…). It builds strength faster. Then lift them all and move to the next string.

::trouble buzzing weak-pinky sore-fingertips
