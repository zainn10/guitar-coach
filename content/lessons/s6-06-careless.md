## The chords

Dm7, Gm7, B♭maj7, A7. Two barre chords, a soft Dm7 and a tense A7 that pulls back to the start.

## Feel

Smooth and steady, with the "universal" strum pattern played lightly. Let the A7 build tension before returning to Dm7.

::strum D-DU-UDU bpm=70 chords=Dm7,Gm7,Bbmaj7,A7

::song careless-whisper

## In key terms

This progression sits in D minor. A7 is the V chord, borrowing the raised note (C♯) from D harmonic minor. That's why it pulls so strongly home.
