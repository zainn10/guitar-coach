## Step 1: find the root

Play any recording you like. Hum the bass note that feels like the centre. Find it on the 6th or 5th string (you know their notes from Stage 4). That's usually the key.

## Step 2: major or minor?

Play both the major and minor chord on that note while the song plays. One will fit, the other will clash.

::listen A label=Hear_A
::listen Am label=Hear_Am
::listen Em label=Mystery_chord

## Step 3: guess the next chord

Most songs use the I, IV, V and vi chords of their key (last lesson). Try those first. You'll be surprised how often they fit.

## Train your ear daily

The **Ear training** tab in Practice plays chords and asks "major or minor?". A minute a day makes a real difference.

::tip Ear training is a slow-and-steady skill. Don't worry if it feels like guessing at first; it really does improve with repetition.
