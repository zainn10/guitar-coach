## Five notes, one box

The minor pentatonic has five notes. Laid out on the neck, they form a "box" your hand can stay in. Two notes per string.

::scale am-pentatonic

## Fingering

Index finger on the 5th fret for every string. Ring finger for the 7th fret; little finger for the 8th. One finger per fret, hand still.

## Move it, change the key

Slide the same box up two frets (index on the 7th fret) and you get **B minor pentatonic**, the scale of the Hotel California solo.

::scale bm-pentatonic

## Practise it

1. Up and down slowly with the metronome, one note per click.
2. Alternate the right hand: i-m-i-m (or down-up with a pick).
3. Use the tempo ladder idea: only speed up when it's clean twice.

::metronome bpm=70
