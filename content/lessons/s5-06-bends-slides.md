## Bends

Push the string across the fret (towards the ceiling on strings 3, 2 and 1) to raise its pitch, like a singer sliding up to a note.

::bend

- Bend with the **ring finger**, with the middle and index fingers behind it on the same string helping.
- The power comes from **turning the wrist**, like turning a key, not from the fingertip.
- **Aim for a pitch**: a half step (one fret higher) or a whole step (two frets). Play the target note first to hear where you're going.

::tip Nylon strings are thick and stretchy: they need more push and won't bend as far as steel strings. Half-step bends are natural on nylon; whole steps take more effort. The check uses a half step.

## Slides

Play a note, keep pressing, and slide the finger along the string to another fret. The note glides up or down without being plucked again. Keep the pressure steady or the note dies.

::end

## Vibrato

A small, regular wobble in pitch on a held note: rock the finger slightly along the string (classical style) or push it gently up and down across the string (rock style). It makes notes sound alive.

::goal These three techniques are all over the Hotel California solo. Get the solo itself from a licensed source; you're building the skills here.
