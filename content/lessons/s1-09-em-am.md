## Move as a unit

Watch the animation. The middle and ring fingers keep their shape and just shift across one string. The index finger drops in for Am and lifts off for Em.

::change Em Am

## The one-minute change drill

This is the single most effective exercise for learning chord changes:

1. Strum Em once.
2. Change to Am and strum once.
3. Back to Em, strum. Keep going for 60 seconds.
4. Count the changes. The app counts them for you, and how many were clean.

Do it every day. The goal for this pair is **30 clean changes in a minute**. It sounds a lot, but most people get there within a week or two of daily minutes.

::tip Don't wait until the chord is perfect before you strum. Speed comes first; accuracy follows surprisingly quickly.

## Your first play-along

**Two Friends** is 8 bars, one strum per bar at a slow 60 beats per minute. You strum on "1", then use beats 2, 3 and 4 to change.

::song two-friends
