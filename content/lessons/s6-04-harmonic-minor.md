## A harmonic minor

The natural A minor scale is A B C D E F G. Raise the G to **G♯** and you get **A harmonic minor**. That one change creates a big gap between F and G♯ and a strong pull up to A: dramatic, Spanish, Middle-Eastern.

::scale a-harmonic-minor

::listen notes=45,47,48,50,52,53,56,57 label=Hear_A_harmonic_minor

## The same notes from E: Phrygian dominant

Start those notes on E and you get flamenco's favourite scale: E F G♯ A B C D E. Play it over an E chord and it sounds instantly flamenco.

::scale e-phrygian

## Why it matters for your goal songs

Minor-key solos and riffs often borrow the raised 7th of harmonic minor, especially when the progression goes to a major V chord (like F♯ in Hotel California, or A7 in Careless Whisper's progression). You've just learned the sound that makes that work.
