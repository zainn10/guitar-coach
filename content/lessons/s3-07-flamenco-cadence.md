::flamenco Four chords stepping down: Am, G, F, E. You've heard it in flamenco, Spanish pop and film soundtracks.

## The Andalusian cadence

Each chord's root note steps down: A, G, F, E. The last step, F to E, is the half-step pull you met in Stage 2.

::chord Am G Fmaj7 E

::listen Am G Fmaj7 E label=Hear_each_chord

## Playing it

We use easy F (Fmaj7) for now; full F comes in Stage 5. Strum with a strong first beat and let E ring at the end, like an arrival.

::strum D-D-D-DU bpm=70 chords=Am,G,Fmaj7,E

::song andalusian-steps

::tip Try playing it slowly with just the thumb, letting each chord ring. Then try it fast and fiery. Same chords, completely different feeling.
