## Patterns come from the pendulum

Keep your arm going **down-up-down-up** in eighth notes, just like before. A pattern is simply which of those movements actually hit the strings. The faint arrows are "misses": your hand still moves, it just doesn't touch the strings.

## Pattern 1: steady fours

::strum D-D-D-D- bpm=75 chords=G,C title=Pattern_1

## Pattern 2: folk

::strum D-DUD-DU bpm=75 chords=G,C title=Pattern_2

## Pattern 3: the "universal" pattern

Down, down-up, (miss), up, down-up. Used in thousands of songs. Say it: "1, 2-and, (3)-and, 4-and".

::strum D-DU-UDU bpm=75 chords=G,C title=Pattern_3

## Pattern 4: waltz (3/4 time)

Some songs count in threes. One strong down-strum then down-up, down-up.

::strum D-DUDU bpm=80 chords=G,C beats=3 title=Pattern_4_(3/4)

::tip Learn each pattern on one chord first. Add the changes only when the pattern runs on autopilot.
