## The "7" sound

A 7th chord adds one extra note that creates a little tension, a sense of wanting to move on. That's what gives blues its flavour.

::chord A7 D7 E7 B7

## B7

The only new four-finger shape. It's the chord that pulls most strongly back to Em, and it's in the capo version of Hotel California.

::build B7

::change B7 Em

## The 12-bar blues

Twelve bars, three chords, the most influential chord form in popular music:

**A7 A7 A7 A7 | D7 D7 A7 A7 | E7 D7 A7 E7**

::strum D-DUD-DU bpm=80 chords=A7,D7,E7

::song twelve-bar-a
