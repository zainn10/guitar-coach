## Chord diagrams

A chord diagram is a picture of the neck standing upright, as if the guitar were hanging on a wall facing you:

::chord Em Am

- **Vertical lines** are the strings: thick low E on the **left**, thin high E on the **right**.
- **Horizontal lines** are frets. The thick line at the top is the nut. Fret numbers are on the left.
- **Coloured dots** are where your fingertips go. The number and colour tell you which finger.
- **O** above a string: play it open. **X**: don't play it.

## The player's view

Chord charts take a little getting used to because they don't match what you see when you look down at your guitar. So every diagram in this app has a **Player's view** switch: the neck lying sideways, headstock on the left, thick string at the **top**, exactly as you see it while playing. Use whichever clicks for you. You can switch in the Chord library or on any chord check.

## Tab

Tab (short for tablature) shows single notes and melodies. Six lines, one per string, with the **thin high E at the top**. A number is the fret to press; 0 means open.

::tab bpm=60 title=Example_tab:_open_strings_then_frets
e|-----------------0---|
B|-------------0-------|
G|---------0-----------|
D|-----0---------------|
A|-0-------------2-----|
E|---------------------|
::end

Read left to right. Press play to hear it.

::tip Tab tells you WHERE to play but not exactly how long each note lasts. In this course, each example has a play button so you can hear the rhythm.
