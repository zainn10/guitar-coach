## Why tune every time

A guitar slightly out of tune makes even perfect chords sound wrong, and it trains your ear badly. Tuning takes 30 seconds with the tuner and should start every practice session.

## How to tune

1. Open the **Tuner** (top menu) and press Start listening.
2. Pluck one string and let it ring.
3. The tuner shows which string it hears and a needle: **left = too low, right = too high**.
4. Turn that string's tuning peg a little, pluck again, watch the needle.
5. When the needle is in the middle and the note turns green, move to the next string.

**Which way?** On most classical guitars, turning the peg so the top of it moves **towards you** (away from the headstock) tightens the string and raises the pitch. If the needle moves the wrong way, turn the other way. Small turns!

::tip Always tune **up** to the note. If you go too high, drop below the note and come back up. The string holds its tuning better.

## New nylon strings stretch

New nylon strings stretch a lot for the first week or two, so they keep drifting flat. That's normal. Gently pull each string away from the fretboard along its length a few times and retune. After a couple of weeks they settle down.

::trouble tuning-drift

## Your check

Tune all six strings. Each one turns green when you hold it in tune for a moment.
