## Little Lantern (an original melody)

Eight notes on the top two strings. Use one finger per fret: index for the 1st fret, ring for the 3rd. Pluck with i and m alternating (index, middle, index, middle…).

::tab bpm=70 per=1 title=Little_Lantern
e|---------0--1--0-----------|
B|-1--3--------------3--1--0-|
G|---------------------------|
D|---------------------------|
A|---------------------------|
E|---------------------------|
::end

## Reading it

- The top line is the thin 1st string.
- Each number is a fret; play them left to right.
- Notice the melody goes up, then comes back down: it's shaped like a little arch.

## Alternate i and m

Never pluck twice in a row with the same finger. Alternating i-m is the foundation of fast single-note playing, and of flamenco **picado** later on.

::tip Say the note names as you play: C, D, E, F, E, D, C, B. You're learning where notes live on the fretboard.
