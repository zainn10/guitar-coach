## What's an arpeggio?

Playing the notes of a chord one at a time instead of all together. Same chord shapes, completely different texture.

## Pattern A: p-i-m-i

::pick p i m i chord=G bpm=66

## Pattern B: p-i-m-a-m-i (up and back down)

::pick p i m a m i chord=C bpm=66

## Pattern C: alternating bass

The thumb alternates between two bass strings while the fingers play together.

::pick p5 im p4 im p5 im p4 im chord=Am bpm=66

## Move the thumb with the chord

The thumb always plays the chord's lowest note: 6th string for G and Em, 5th for C, Am and A, 4th for D and Dm.

::tip Keep the hand still and let the fingers do the work. If your wrist bounces, slow down.

::trouble fingerpicking-coordination
