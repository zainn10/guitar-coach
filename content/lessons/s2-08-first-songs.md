## Playing a song

Songs combine everything: steady strumming, changes on time, and keeping going no matter what.

**The golden rule: never stop strumming.** If the next chord isn't ready, strum anyway: open strings, half a chord, anything. Stopping is what makes songs fall apart.

::strum D-D-D-D- bpm=70 chords=D,A

## Tom Dooley (traditional)

A classic American folk song using only D and A. You'll play the chord progression; four down-strums per bar.

::song tom-dooley

## Tempo ladder

Every song starts slower than its real tempo. After two clean takes in a row, the tempo goes up 5 beats per minute. The song map after each take shows exactly which bars were solid (green), a bit late or messy (amber) or struggling (red). Tap "Fix it" to loop the hard bars slower.

Also unlocked now: **Morning Walk** (A and D) and **Shadows in D minor** (Dm and A).
