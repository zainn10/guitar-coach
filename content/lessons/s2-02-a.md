## A major (A)

Three fingers sit side by side on the **2nd fret** of strings 4, 3 and 2. The open 1st string rings on top. Don't play the low E: start your strum on the 5th string.

::build A

## Fitting three fingers in one fret

They won't all fit right behind the fret wire. That's fine: angle them slightly so each fingertip has its own space, with the ring finger closest to the fret.

**Two fingerings:**
- **1-2-3** (index, middle, ring): the standard, shown above.
- **2-3-4** (middle, ring, little): leaves your index finger free, and some people with wide fingers find it easier.

::tip The 1st string is the one that gets muted in A, by the underside of the ring finger. Use **String by string** in the check to catch it: this is one of the octave strings the app can check even in a strum.

::trouble muted-strings buzzing
