## The musical alphabet

Notes go **A B C D E F G**, then start again. Each fret is one step called a **semitone**. Most natural notes are two frets apart, except **E-F** and **B-C**, which are only one fret apart.

::fretboard 6:0:E 6:1:F 6:3:G 5:0:A 5:2:B 5:3:C 4:0:D 4:2:E 4:3:F from=0 to=4

## Why this matters

These bass notes are the roots of your chords: G's lowest note is the G at the 3rd fret of the 6th string, C's is the C at the 3rd fret of the 5th string. Knowing them helps with fingerpicking (where the thumb goes) and later with barre chords.

::tip The quick quizzes include "find the note" questions from now on, so these names stick.
