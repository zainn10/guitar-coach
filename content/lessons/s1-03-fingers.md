## Left hand: numbers

Your left hand presses the strings (the "fretting hand"). Each finger has a number and a colour that's the same in every diagram in this app:

::hand left

- **1 = index** (blue)
- **2 = middle** (orange)
- **3 = ring** (purple)
- **4 = little** (teal)

The thumb has no number. It stays behind the neck.

## Right hand: letters

Your right hand plays the strings. For strumming you won't need the letters yet, but for fingerpicking and flamenco you will:

::hand right

- **p** = thumb (*pulgar*)
- **i** = index (*índice*)
- **m** = middle (*medio*)
- **a** = ring (*anular*)
- **e** = little finger, used in flamenco rasgueado

::tip Say the numbers out loud the first few times you place a chord: "two on the fifth string, three on the fourth". It wires the names into your hands surprisingly quickly.
