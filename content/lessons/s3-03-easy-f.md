## Fmaj7: F without the barre

Full F needs one finger to press all six strings, which takes weeks to build up to (Stage 5). Fmaj7 gives you a lovely F sound right now, with three fingers in a diagonal line on the top four strings.

::build Fmaj7

## A stepping stone

Your index and middle fingers are already in the exact spots they'll use in full F. Every time you play Fmaj7, you're preparing for it.

::change C Fmaj7

::listen Fmaj7
