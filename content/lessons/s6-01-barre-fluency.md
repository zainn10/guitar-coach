## More moveable shapes

Every open shape can become a barre chord. Here are the ones you'll need for Careless Whisper's progression and beyond:

::chord Gm Gm7 Bbmaj7 Bb Dm7

## Gm7: the friendly barre

A full barre at the 3rd fret plus one finger. Great for building barre stamina.

::build Gm7

## Change without releasing everything

Between two barre chords, **relax the pressure but keep the hand shape** as you move, then press again on arrival. Don't lift the whole hand off the neck.

::change Bm Fsharp

::change Gm7 Bbmaj7

## Drill

One minute of Bm ⇄ F♯ (goal: 15 clean changes). Barre changes are slower than open ones; 15 is a strong start.

::trouble barre-dead hand-ache
