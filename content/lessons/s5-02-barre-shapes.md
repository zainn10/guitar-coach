## The big idea

A barre chord is an open chord shape where your index finger replaces the nut. Because nothing is open, you can **slide the whole shape** anywhere: every fret is a different chord.

## The E shape (root on the 6th string)

F is the E shape at the 1st fret. Move it up one fret and it's F♯; to the 3rd fret, G.

::chord F Fsharp

## The A shape and the Am shape (root on the 5th string)

Bm is the Am shape with a barre at the 2nd fret. B is the A shape at the 2nd fret.

::chord Bm B

::build Bm

## Naming them

The chord is named after the note under your index finger on the root string (6th for E-shape, 5th for A-shape). That's why you learned the bass notes in Stage 4.

::tip F♯ and Bm are both in Hotel California's progression. You're two chords away from playing it in the original key.

::trouble barre-dead f-chord
