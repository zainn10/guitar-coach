## Greensleeves

A traditional English tune over 400 years old. You'll play its chords as a fingerpicked accompaniment: p-i-m-a-m-i on each chord, six notes per bar in 3/4 time.

::pick p i m a m i chord=Am bpm=60

::chord Am G E

## Tips

- The thumb finds each new bass note while the fingers keep their home strings.
- Change chords during the last two notes (m-i) of each bar.
- Smooth and even beats fast. The app checks your timing.

::song greensleeves
