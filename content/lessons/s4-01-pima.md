## Home strings

In fingerpicking each right-hand finger has a "home":

- **p** (thumb): the bass strings, 4, 5 or 6, whichever is the chord's lowest note.
- **i** (index): 3rd string.
- **m** (middle): 2nd string.
- **a** (ring): 1st string.

::hand right

## Free stroke

Pluck by bending the finger at the knuckle so it moves **into the palm**, passing just over the next string. The thumb plucks **down and slightly forward**, staying in front of the fingers so they don't collide.

## Your first pattern: p-i-m-a

Hold Am. Thumb on the 5th string, then index, middle, ring on strings 3, 2, 1. One note per half-beat, evenly.

::pick p i m a chord=Am bpm=60

::tip Plant first: rest p, i, m and a lightly on their strings, then play them one by one. It anchors the hand and stops fingers hitting wrong strings.
