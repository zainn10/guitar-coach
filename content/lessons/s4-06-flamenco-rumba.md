::flamenco The rumba flamenca is one of the most fun and accessible flamenco styles: a bright 4/4 groove with a percussive chuck.

## The chuck (X)

A **chuck** is a short percussive hit: on the down-stroke, let the palm or fingertips land on the strings and stop them instantly. You hear a "chk" instead of a chord.

## The rumba pattern

Down, (chuck), up, (chuck), up, down. Count: "1 &, 2 &, 3 &, 4 &".

::strum D-XU-XUD bpm=80 chords=Am,G,Fmaj7,E

## Thumb technique (pulgar)

Flamenco guitarists use the thumb a lot, for strong bass notes and for brushed strums:

- **Rest stroke**: the thumb plays a bass string and comes to rest on the next string. Strong and full.
- **Thumb strum**: brush down across the strings with the side of the thumb, slow and warm.

Try the Andalusian cadence with slow thumb strums, then with the rumba pattern.

::song rumba-sol

::tip Rumba lives in the bounce, not the speed. Accent the chucks and keep it relaxed.
