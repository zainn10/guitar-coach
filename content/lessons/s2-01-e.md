## From Em to E

You already know E minor. Add your **index finger on the 3rd string, 1st fret** and it becomes **E major**. That single note changes the mood from thoughtful to bright.

::chord Em E

::build E

## Hear the difference

::listen Em label=Hear_E_minor
::listen E label=Hear_E_major

Major chords tend to sound bright and settled; minor chords darker. Your ear will learn this quickly, and it's tested in the ear-training quizzes.

::tip E is the "home" chord of flamenco. You'll meet it again at the end of this stage.
