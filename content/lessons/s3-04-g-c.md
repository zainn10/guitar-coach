## Why it's hard

Every finger moves, and your hand shifts across the neck. Almost every beginner finds this change slow at first. It's normal, and it gets fast with daily drilling.

::change G C

## Break it down

1. **Look at the pattern**: middle and index fingers move from strings 6-5 (G) to strings 5-4 (C): the same shape, one string over.
2. **Move in order**: ring finger first (it travels furthest), then middle, then index.
3. **In the air**: lift, land the whole C shape at once. Ten times without strumming.
4. **Then the drill**: one minute, count your changes. The goal is 25 clean changes a minute.

::tip In songs, start moving on beat 4 of the bar before. If you're not ready, strum the open strings on beat 4. Nobody will notice.
