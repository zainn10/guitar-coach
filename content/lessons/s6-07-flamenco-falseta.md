::flamenco A **falseta** is a short melodic passage a flamenco guitarist plays between sung verses or dance sections. Every player has their own.

## Your first falseta (original)

A descending phrase in E Phrygian that ends on low E, home. Play it with the thumb (rest strokes) for a strong, dark tone.

::tab bpm=70 per=2 title=Falseta_(original)
e|---------------------------|
B|---------------------------|
G|---------------------------|
D|-2--3--2-------------------|
A|----------3--2--0----------|
E|-------------------1--0----|
::end

Notice the F (1st fret, 6th string) falling to E at the end: the Phrygian pull you've known since Stage 2.

## Tangos

Tangos is a joyful 4/4 flamenco style. Over the Andalusian cadence, use this groove with an accent on beats 2 and 4 (or with a rasgueado on beat 1 when you're comfortable):

::strum D-DUD-DU bpm=90 chords=Am,G,F,E

::song tangos-del-sol

## Make it yours

Change one note of the falseta, or its rhythm. Play it, then strum the tangos groove. That's the basic shape of flamenco playing: compás, falseta, compás.
