## Anchor and guide fingers

The fastest changes move as few fingers as possible:

- An **anchor finger** stays exactly where it is (gold ring in the animations). Never lift it.
- A **guide finger** slides along the same string to its new fret (dashed gold ring). Keep it lightly touching the string as it moves.

## The drills

Two one-minute drills: **A ⇄ D** and **A ⇄ E**. Goal: 30 clean changes in a minute for each. Do them daily; your scores are graphed on the Progress page.

::tip Practise the change "in the air": lift the whole hand a centimetre and land the new shape all at once, without strumming. Ten times. Then add the strum.
