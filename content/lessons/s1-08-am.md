## A minor (Am)

A minor uses three fingers and five strings: **don't play the thick low E**. Start your strum on the 5th string (A).

::build Am

## Spot the pattern

Look at Em and Am side by side. The middle and ring fingers make the **same shape**, just moved over one string (from strings 5-4 to strings 4-3). Then the index finger joins on the 2nd string, 1st fret.

::chord Em Am

::listen Am

## The usual problem

The index finger on the 1st fret often lies flat and mutes the open 1st string. Curl it so only the tip touches the 2nd string.

::trouble muted-strings wrong-strings
