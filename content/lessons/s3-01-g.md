## G major (G)

G covers the whole neck: the 6th string at the 3rd fret, the 5th at the 2nd, the 1st at the 3rd, and three open strings in the middle.

::build G

## Making the stretch

- Drop your thumb lower behind the neck: it opens the hand.
- Place the **ring finger first** (it reaches furthest), then middle, then index.
- The open 4th, 3rd and 2nd strings must ring: arch the fingers so their undersides don't touch them.

::mistake thumb

::listen G

::trouble stretch muted-strings
