## Counting "and"

Between the beats there's an "and": **1 and 2 and 3 and 4 and**. Down-strums go on the numbers, up-strums on the "and".

::strum DUDUDUDU bpm=60 chords=A,D

## The pendulum

Your arm is a pendulum that **never stops**: down, up, down, up, evenly. Down-strums are a bit stronger and hit the bass strings; up-strums are lighter and only catch the top three or four strings.

- With bare fingers on nylon: down with the back of the index fingernail, up with the fleshy side of the thumb or the index pad.
- Keep the wrist loose. Tension makes it sound choppy.

::tip Up-strums are naturally quieter, so the app is forgiving if it misses one. Keep the arm moving and the timing even.

::trouble upstrums stiff-strumming losing-beat
