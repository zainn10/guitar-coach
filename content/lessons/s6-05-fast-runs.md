## Speed is controlled accuracy

Fast runs come from **small, relaxed, efficient movements** repeated at a speed you can play cleanly, then nudged faster. Never practise faster than you can play clean.

## The run

An original descending figure in A minor, crossing from the 1st to the 4th string:

::tab bpm=80 per=2 title=Descending_run_(original)
e|-8--5--------------------|
B|-------8--6--5-----------|
G|----------------7--5-----|
D|----------------------7--|
A|-------------------------|
E|-------------------------|
::end

## Technique

- **Right hand**: alternate i-m (flamenco *picado*) or down-up with a pick. Never the same finger twice in a row.
- **Left hand**: keep fingers close to the strings. Lift each finger only just enough.
- **String crossings**: plan which finger plays first on each new string so alternation continues.

## Use the ladder

1. Metronome at 60, two notes per click. Play it 3 times clean.
2. Up 5 bpm. Repeat. When it gets messy, drop back 10 and climb again.
3. Track your best clean tempo each week.
