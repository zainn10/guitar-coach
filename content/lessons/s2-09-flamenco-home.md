::flamenco In flamenco, E isn't just a chord: it's home, the place every phrase returns to.

## The flamenco "F"

Make an E chord, then slide all three fingers **up one fret**, leaving the open strings ringing. That's not a textbook F major, but it's the sound flamenco guitarists love: a slightly clashing chord that pulls strongly back down to E.

::chord E Fflam

::change E Fflam

::listen E Fflam label=Hear_E_then_up_a_fret

## Why it works

The note F is just one fret above E. That half-step pull down to E is the signature of the **Phrygian mode**, the flamenco scale. You'll learn the full scale in Stage 6.

## Play it with feeling

Strum E, then the F colour, then back to E. Let it ring. Try a stronger strum on the F and a softer one back on E, like a sigh.

::song flamenco-home

::tip Flamenco players often play this with the thumb alone, slowly brushing down the strings. Try it.
