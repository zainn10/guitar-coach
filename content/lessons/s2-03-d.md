## D major (D)

D uses the **top four strings only** (strings 4 to 1). Your three fingers make a small triangle.

::build D

## Aim your strum

Hitting the low E or A strings is the most common D mistake: it makes the chord muddy. Rest your strumming thumb on the 4th string before you strum, and start there.

::mistake strum-arm

::listen D
