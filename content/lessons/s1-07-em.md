## E minor (Em)

E minor is one of the easiest and most useful chords on the guitar: two fingers, all six strings ring. It sounds a little sad and thoughtful (that's what "minor" sounds like).

::build Em

## Strum it

With your right thumb (or the back of your index fingernail), brush down across all six strings, from the thickest to the thinnest. Slowly is fine. Let it ring.

::listen Em

## Check each string

A chord is only clean if every string rings. Strum, then pluck each string one at a time from the 6th to the 1st. If one sounds dead, a finger is touching it: usually the underside of your ring finger leaning on the open 3rd string.

::mistake finger-flat

::tip Your check below listens to you. Use **String by string** mode if you want to check every string individually.
