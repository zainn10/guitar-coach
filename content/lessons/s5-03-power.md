## Root and fifth

A power chord is just two notes (a root and the note five steps up), often with the root doubled on top. With no third, it's neither major nor minor: a strong, open sound.

::chord E5 A5 G5 C5

## The moveable shape

Index finger on the root (6th or 5th string), ring and little fingers two frets higher on the next two strings. Slide it anywhere: the root note names the chord.

## Keeping other strings quiet

Let the underside of your index finger lightly touch the strings you don't play. Strum only the three strings you need.

## Eighth-note down-strokes

::strum DDDDDDDD bpm=90 chords=E5,G5,A5,A5

::tip On nylon, power chords sound gentle rather than heavy. That's fine: the shape and the rhythmic precision are what you're building.
