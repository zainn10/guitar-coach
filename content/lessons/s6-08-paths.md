## Look how far you've come

From your first two-finger chord to barre chords, fingerpicking, scales, bends, fast runs and flamenco rhythms. Your Progress page shows the whole journey.

## Three paths

- **Flamenco**: compás in 12 (soleá, bulerías), rasgueado variations, picado scales, alzapúa thumb technique and longer falsetas.
- **Fingerstyle & classical**: arpeggio studies, melody with bass, public-domain classical pieces like Spanish Romance.
- **Songs & lead**: pentatonic positions across the neck, phrasing with bends and vibrato, and soloing over progressions, including the Hotel California solo from a licensed source.

Choose one on the **Course** page (bottom). Your sessions keep reviewing everything you've learned, and the goal-piece tracker on the Progress page shows what's left for your goal songs.

::tip Whichever path you choose, keep the daily habit: weakest thing first, then something new, then music you love.
