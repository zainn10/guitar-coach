## The beat

Almost all the music you'll play has a steady pulse, the **beat**, grouped into **bars** of four: **1, 2, 3, 4, 1, 2, 3, 4**. Beat 1 is a little stronger.

## Down-strums on every beat

Strum down once on every beat. Count out loud: "1, 2, 3, 4". Tap your foot.

::strum D-D-D-D- bpm=70 chords=Em,Am

- Strum from the **elbow**, with a loose wrist. Small movement.
- Your arm goes down on each beat and comes back up in between, **without** touching the strings.
- Aim for an **even** volume and an even gap between strums.

::mistake strum-arm

::tip If you drift ahead of the click, you're rushing. Try to make the click "disappear" under your strum.

## Your check

Four bars of down-strums on Em at 70 beats per minute. The app checks that your strums land on the beats.

::trouble losing-beat stiff-strumming
