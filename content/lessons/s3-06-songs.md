## The four-chord trick

G, Em, C and D in this order turn up in an enormous number of songs. Learn this progression and you can strum along to a lot of music.

::chord G Em C D

## Campfire Four

The universal pattern over G-Em-C-D. Start slow with the tempo ladder.

::song campfire-four
