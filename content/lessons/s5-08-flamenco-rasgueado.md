::flamenco Rasgueado is flamenco's signature: a rolling, percussive strum made by fingers flicking out from a loose fist.

## Single-finger flick

Curl your fingers into a loose fist above the strings, thumb resting on the 6th string. Flick the **index finger** out so its nail strikes down across the strings. Return. Repeat evenly with the metronome.

::rasgueado i chord=E

## The 4-stroke rasgueado: e-a-m-i

Little finger, ring, middle, index, each flicking out in turn. Even spacing is everything.

::rasgueado four chord=E

::rasgueado three chord=E

## Practise it

1. Each finger alone, 10 flicks.
2. The sequence **very** slowly: one finger per click.
3. The check: e-a-m-i spread evenly across beat 1 (one stroke per quarter of the beat), then single down-strokes on beats 2, 3 and 4, at a slow 50 bpm.

::tip Honest limit: the app checks rasgueado for the chord and the timing of the strokes, not each individual finger.
