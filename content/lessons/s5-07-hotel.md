::goal This is the progression behind one of the most famous guitar solos. Chord progression only: no melody, solo or lyrics. Get the solo from a licensed tab book or lesson when you're ready.

## Capo version

Put a capo on the **7th fret**. Now the easy shapes Em, B7, D, A, C, G, Am, B7 sound in the original key.

::chord Em B7 D A C G Am B7

::song hotel-california-capo

## Original key

No capo: Bm, F♯, A, E, G, D, Em, F♯. Two barre chords (Bm and F♯), the rest open.

::chord Bm Fsharp A E G D Em Fsharp

::change Bm Fsharp

::song hotel-california
